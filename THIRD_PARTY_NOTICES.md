## PIE

Pedestrian Intention Estimation (PIE):

- Official repository: https://github.com/aras62/PIE
- Dataset page: https://data.nvision2.eecs.yorku.ca/PIE_dataset/
- The official repository states that the project is released under the MIT License; the dataset page also provides the original citation requirements.

Please cite:

A. Rasouli, I. Kotseruba, T. Kunic, and J. K. Tsotsos, “PIE: A Large-Scale Dataset and Models for Pedestrian Intention Estimation and Trajectory Prediction,” ICCV, 2019.

## JAAD

Joint Attention in Autonomous Driving (JAAD):

- Official repository: https://github.com/ykotseruba/JAAD
- Dataset page: https://data.nvision2.eecs.yorku.ca/JAAD_dataset/
- The official JAAD repository releases its annotations/interface under the MIT License and lists the video clips under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Please cite:

A. Rasouli, I. Kotseruba, and J. K. Tsotsos, “Are They Going to Cross? A Benchmark Dataset and Baseline for Pedestrian Crosswalk Behavior,” ICCVW, 2017.
